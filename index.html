Our story

Built on slow mornings and neighborly tables.

Aristan started as a tiny weekend roastery. We wanted a place where the coffee mattered, but people mattered more. Our baristas train with care, our bakers arrive before sunrise, and we invite the community to linger.

Every bag is roasted in small batches, cupped for sweetness, and dialed in daily. We believe in traceable sourcing, fair relationships, and coffee that tastes as good as it feels.
